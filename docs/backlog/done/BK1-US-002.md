# BK1-US-002 — Regulatory obligations mapped to requirement IDs

**Workflow ID:** BK1
**Phase:** 1 — Discovery & Requirements Sign-off
**Status:** Done (retrospective record)
**Story points:** L

---

**As a** Compliance Officer,
**I want** every applicable regulatory obligation (GDPR, EU AI Act, ISO 42001, MiFID II/EMIR, NIST AI RMF, SOX/SOC 2) mapped to a requirement ID before build starts,
**so that** compliance review happens against the design, not retrofitted after the workflow is built.

**Traces to:** GDPR-Art6-001, GDPR-Art5-001, GDPR-Art5-002, GDPR-Art35-001, GDPR-Art17-001, GDPR-Art44-001, EUAIACT-CLASS-001, EUAIACT-LR-001, EUAIACT-HR-001, EUAIACT-LOG-001, ISO42001-POL-001, ISO42001-RISK-001, ISO42001-LC-001, ISO42001-CI-001, MIFID-ART25-001, MIFID-ART25-002, MIFID-BEST-001, EMIR-001, NIST-GOV-001, NIST-MAP-001, NIST-MEAS-001, NIST-MANAGE-001, SOX-IC-001, SOX-AT-001, SOC2-SEC-001, SOC2-AV-001 (`requirements.md` §7)

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Given `requirements.md` §7, When each regulatory ID is checked, Then each has an explicit "Applicability" verdict (Applicable / Not applicable / Conditional / Assess at go-live) — none left ambiguous.
- Given the EU AI Act classification (EUAIACT-CLASS-001), When the LLM's role is checked against Annex III, Then the provisional classification (Limited-risk) is stated with its reasoning, not just asserted.

**Definition of Ready:** Regulatory scope for a custody/asset-servicing domain confirmed (GDPR, EU AI Act, ISO 42001, MiFID II/EMIR, NIST AI RMF, SOX/SOC 2 — no other frameworks in scope for MVP).

**Definition of Done:** §7 committed with an applicability verdict per ID; no ID left without a verdict.
